How Soultri started

Every good thing starts with a small thought. Soultri was one of them — a dream, a tiny shutter, and a lot of hope.

The dream

For years, we would sit together talking about food, desserts, and the dream of building something of our own someday — something that genuinely reflected our love for good food and creativity.

Back then, bakeries felt like a luxury to us. Visiting one was rare, maybe once a month if we were lucky. But somewhere in those small moments, little Momo quietly developed a deep love for baking and pastry.

Chef Momo

That passion grew stronger over the years. While pursuing engineering, Momo made the difficult decision to leave that path and move into hotel management instead.

Around seven years ago, he joined a well-known bakery in Hyderabad and spent years learning under talented chefs and pastry teams — not just baking, but the small details behind pastry, desserts, consistency and quality.

Chef Momo was one of the early people behind introducing the San Sebastián Cheesecake in Hyderabad, quietly working behind the scenes without much recognition. A true underdog story.

“Why don't we finally build something of our own?”

Building it

That one conversation between Bobo, Juju and Momo slowly became the beginning of Soultri. But good things take time. It took almost a year to put everything together — budget issues, finding the right place, planning the kitchen.

We compromised on many things. Fancy interiors, aesthetic café setups and expensive décor could wait. But one thing we never wanted to compromise on was quality.

Our goal was simple: make genuinely good desserts with quality ingredients, at prices people can afford regularly. So we started small — a tiny shutter, a simple setup, and a lot of hope.

The people behind the characters

We created Bobo, Juju and Chef Momo not to hide ourselves, but to bring a little fun and personality into Soultri. Growing up watching cartoons and admiring creativity, we wanted the brand to feel playful, warm and memorable.

If you're curious about the real people behind the characters, come visit Soultri. We'll probably be there. Soultri is still at the beginning of its journey, but we truly believe honest work, consistency and good food will slowly take us forward.

Good to know

Do you have eggless options?

Yes. Brownies, cookies, custard, chocolate desserts and cakes have eggless versions, and the menu marks which items contain egg.

How do I order?

Message us on WhatsApp, or walk in at the shop. For cakes, gifting and bulk orders, share your date and idea and we'll take it from there.

Why do some desserts take 10–15 minutes?

Our chocolate and custard desserts are prepared fresh when you order, so they're at their best.

Do you do custom cakes and bulk orders?

Yes. Send us the occasion, size and date on WhatsApp and we'll discuss the details with you.
